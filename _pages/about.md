I am a Master's student in **Robotics, Systems and Control** at **ETH Zurich**. I work at
the boundary between *classical methods* — control, estimation, and optimization — and
*machine learning*, building principled robotics systems and deploying them on real
hardware.

Right now I am an AI Engineering Intern on the perception team at
[Flexion Robotics](https://flexion.ai/about), where I develop learned proprioceptive
state estimation to complement a classical localization-and-mapping pipeline.

Before that, in Raffaello D'Andrea's group at ETH, I was a main contributor to the
perception pipeline behind [Antonio Terpin](https://antonioterpin.com/)'s
[real-time PIV for fluid control](https://activefluidcontrol.com/team) — developing learning-based and
optimization-based methods that advance the accuracy–speed Pareto front.

I did my BSc in Automation and Control Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.

I'm always happy to talk about robotics, learning, and where classical methods meet ML —
feel free to [reach out](mailto:fbanelli@ethz.ch).
